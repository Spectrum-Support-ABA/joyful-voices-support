Delete Your Account

Last Updated:

⚠️ Important: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account will:

  • Permanently delete all your account data
  • Remove all synced communication boards and settings
  • Cancel any active subscriptions (no refund for unused time)
  • Delete your email and authentication credentials

Before you delete: Consider exporting your data if you want to keep a backup of your communication boards.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Joyful Voices account using either of these methods:

Method 1: Delete Within the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Joyful Voices app on your device
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Scroll to the Account section
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm your decision by entering your password
  6. Tap "Permanently Delete"

Processing time: Your account will be deleted immediately, and all data will be removed from our systems within 30 days.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer email, send a deletion request to:

Email: spectrumjfj@gmail.com

Include in your email:

  • Subject line: "Delete My Account"
  • The email address associated with your account
  • Confirmation that you understand this action is permanent

Processing time: We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation email once complete.

What Happens to Your Subscription?

Apple App Store Subscriptions:

Deleting your account does NOT automatically cancel your subscription. To avoid future charges:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone/iPad
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find Joyful Voices
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

Note: Subscriptions are managed by Apple. No refunds are provided for unused subscription time after account deletion.

Data Retention

After account deletion:

  • Immediate: Your account is deactivated and you cannot log in
  • Within 30 days: All personal information is permanently deleted from active systems
  • Up to 90 days: Backup copies may remain for disaster recovery, then are permanently deleted
  • Legal retention: We may retain certain information if required by law (e.g., for tax purposes)

Changed Your Mind?

If you're experiencing issues with the app, consider these alternatives before deleting your account:

  • Contact Support: We may be able to resolve your issue (spectrumjfj@gmail.com)
  • Pause Subscription: Cancel your subscription but keep your account and data
  • Export Data: Save your communication boards before deciding
